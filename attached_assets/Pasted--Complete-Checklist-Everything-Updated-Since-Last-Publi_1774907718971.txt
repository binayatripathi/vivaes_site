Pricing Architecture — Before vs. Now
Before (old system): The engine had a detailed cost table for every service with separate line items for Labor, Materials, Equipment, Site Prep, and Permits — each with a base cost and a per-unit rate. It multiplied these together and showed customers an exact dollar total (e.g. "Labor: $1,200 / Materials: $800 / Equipment: $5,500 → Total: $8,647"). This was misleading because no licensed contractor can commit to those numbers without a site visit.
Now (new system): The engine uses a single starting price per service and applies multipliers to produce an honest range:
Service	Starting From
EV Chargers	$850
Panel Upgrades	$3,500
Re-Roofing + Panel Removal	$3,600
Electrification Assessment	$250 (fixed)
General Electrical	$600
Lighting Retrofits	$600
Commercial	$600
Battery Add-On (Existing Solar)	$15,500
Battery Backup (No Solar)	$15,500
Solar + Battery (New)	$30,000
Solar & Storage (general)	$30,000
EV + Panel + Battery Bundle	$28,000
The multipliers applied on top:
* Project size: Small = 1×, Medium = 1.6×, Large = 2.5× (re-roofing scales differently by panel count)
* Property type: Residential = 1×, Commercial = 1.35×, Industrial = 1.7×
* Urgency: Standard = 1×, Priority = 1.15×, Emergency = 1.4×
* Battery capacity: 5 kW = 1×, 10 kW = 1.8×, 13.5 kW = 2.3×, 15 kW = 2.6×
* EV charger level: 15A = 0.7×, 32A = 1×, 50A = 1.25×, 60A = 1.45×
* Wiring distance: Short = 1×, Medium = 1.2×, Long = 1.5×
* Panel upgrade add-on: 200A = +$3,500 / 400A = +$5,500
* Permits: Always minimum $500, capped at 2% of subtotal
* Large project discount: −5% applied automatically
* Variance buffer: ±15% for electrical, ±20% for solar/battery (shown as low–high range)
The result is displayed as "$X,XXX – $X,XXX and up · depending on site conditions" — never a fake exact total. The customer sees a realistic starting range and knows the final number comes after an on-site inspection.
